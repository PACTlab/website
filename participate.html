Current study

Precision Diagnostics for Hearing Loss

Do you have trouble hearing? The Precision Audiology and Clinical Translation Lab is looking for adults with or without diagnosed hearing loss to participate in a research study. This study aims to improve how hearing problems are diagnosed and to better understand why some people have difficulty hearing in noisy places. Compensation is provided.

The purpose of this study is to better understand how changes in the inner ear affect hearing and to develop improved tools to detect these changes. We are especially interested in learning which types of tests best capture differences in hearing and how those results relate to real-world listening challenges.

Is this study right for you?

  • Adults aged 18 years or older
  • Have hearing difficulties or think you may have trouble hearing
  • Able to complete approximately 3 hours of testing over 2–4 visits

What does participation involve?

  • A standard hearing test (audiogram)
  • Non-invasive physiological measurements of hearing function measured from brainwaves or the ear
  • Speech-in-noise testing and hearing questionnaires
  • Compensation provided for your time

Approved by the University of Pittsburgh IRB (STUDY26030005 Precision Diagnostics)
